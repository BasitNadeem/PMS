## Long description (~1,400 characters)

```
Innflo is an all-in-one property management system for independent hotels, guesthouses, hostels, resorts, serviced apartments and glamping sites.

Most small properties run on a mix of paper registers, spreadsheets and group chats. Bookings get lost, the front desk never knows which rooms are actually clean, guest bills are added up by hand at checkout, and owners have no reliable view of how the business is performing. Innflo replaces that with one connected system.

Front desk staff manage every reservation in one list — walk-ins, phone bookings, travel agents and direct website bookings alike — and check guests in and out from a single screen. Charges from the room, the restaurant and other services collect automatically into an itemised folio that is ready the moment a guest asks to settle.

Housekeeping staff update room status from their own phones, including offline where connectivity is poor, and the front desk sees it the moment a signal returns. Checking a guest out creates the next cleaning task automatically.

Properties with food service can run a restaurant point of sale with guest QR-code ordering and a live kitchen display, posting charges straight to the guest's room.

Each property gets its own branded booking engine on its own web address, so direct reservations arrive without commission to a third party.

Owners and managers get over 25 built-in reports covering revenue, occupancy, ADR, guests, staff activity and stock.

Innflo is built with Pakistani properties in mind, including local tax configuration and locally used payment methods.
```

## Pricing — decide before you start

Public pricing is currently withheld on the website, but **most directories
rank and filter by price, and listings without one convert noticeably worse.**
Prospects filter for a budget band and a "contact us" listing simply never
appears.

Two options:

1. **Publish a starting price** — e.g. "Starting from PKR X/month per property",
   with the entry tier only. Best for discovery.
2. **Contact for pricing** — consistent with the site today, but expect fewer
   directory-sourced leads.

**NEEDS FOUNDER INPUT.** Pick one and keep it consistent across all four
directories; conflicting prices across listings look careless and are easy for a
prospect to spot.

## Do not add

These are not shipped. Listing them creates a promise the demo cannot keep, and
directory reviews punish exactly that.

- **Channel manager / OTA sync** (Booking.com, Agoda, Expedia, Airbnb) — in
  development, not live
- **WhatsApp messaging or automated guest messaging** — not connected to a live
  provider
- **SMS notifications** — same
- **Online payment collection / card checkout in the booking engine** — payments
  are recorded by staff, not processed online
- **Multi-property or chain management** — one property per account today
- **Any security certification** (SOC 2, ISO, PCI) or compliance claim
- **Customer counts, ratings or testimonials** you cannot evidence
