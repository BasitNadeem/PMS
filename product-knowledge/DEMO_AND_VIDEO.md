# Demo & Video Guidance

Guidance for building product demos and short Meta/Instagram/Facebook explainer videos for InnFlo. This identifies WHAT to show, not final scripted ad copy.

---

# Best Features to Show Visually

1. **QR-code guest food ordering** — guest scans a code, orders on their phone, order appears instantly on the kitchen screen. Very visual, intuitive without narration, proves speed and modernity.
2. **Channel manager auto-sync** — a booking made on an OTA (or simulated) appearing automatically inside InnFlo with availability updated. Proves the "never get overbooked" promise.
3. **Direct booking website** — a guest browsing rooms with photos and instantly seeing prices update as extras are added. Proves the commission-free booking site is real and polished.
4. **Housekeeping mobile app** — a staff member marking a room clean on their phone, with the front-desk screen updating in real time. Proves mobile, real-world usability.
5. **Reservations screen with all sources in one list** — walk-in, OTA, direct site bookings sitting together. Proves centralization.
6. **Guest folio building up in real time** — charges from different departments landing on one bill. Proves the "no more paper slips" benefit.
7. **Reports/dashboard** — a visually rich chart or report (occupancy trend, revenue by source) populated with realistic data. Proves business visibility.
8. **Shift handover cash reconciliation** — quick, clean screen showing balanced cash. Good for the "trust and accountability" angle.

# Best Product Screens / Views

- **Booking Engine landing page** (`BookingLandingPage`) — room browsing with photos/amenities and a live cart. Action: pick dates, browse rooms, add an extra. Benefit: commission-free, modern direct booking.
- **Reservations list** — Action: show a new booking appearing (from an OTA or a walk-in being entered). Benefit: everything in one place.
- **Guest Folio** — Action: show charges accumulating and the checkout total. Benefit: accurate, effortless billing.
- **Housekeeping Mobile App** — Action: mark a room clean on a phone. Benefit: works for frontline staff, even offline.
- **Kitchen Display** — Action: an order arriving live after a QR order is placed. Benefit: speed and accuracy.
- **Channel Manager page** — Action: show connected OTAs and sync status. Benefit: peace of mind against overbooking.
- **A report page** (e.g., Occupancy Trend or Revenue Source) — Action: scroll/filter a chart. Benefit: business intelligence at a glance.
- **Dashboard/home screen** — Action: quick overview glance. Benefit: "everything at a glance" opening shot.

# Best Demo Workflows

## Workflow: Guest Books Direct, No Commission
Starting screen: Booking Engine landing page
Action: Pick dates, browse rooms, add an extra (e.g., breakfast), enter guest details
Next screen/action: Submit booking → confirmation screen/email
Result: A confirmed reservation appears instantly in the hotel's InnFlo dashboard
Customer benefit: A booking captured with zero commission paid to a third party
Suggested screen-recording sequence: Booking site (dates → room → cart) → submit → cut to InnFlo reservations list showing the new booking appear

## Workflow: OTA Booking Auto-Syncs, No Overbooking
Starting screen: Booking.com (or a mocked OTA booking confirmation)
Action: A guest completes a booking on the OTA
Next screen/action: Cut to InnFlo reservations list — the booking appears automatically
Result: Availability across all other OTAs updates instantly
Customer benefit: Never accidentally double-book a room again
Suggested screen-recording sequence: OTA booking confirmation → InnFlo reservation list (booking appears, tagged with its source) → Channel Manager screen showing synced status

## Workflow: Front Desk Check-In to Checkout
Starting screen: Reservations list
Action: Check a guest in, assign a room
Next screen/action: Add a restaurant charge to the guest's folio; later, check out and view total bill
Result: A clean, itemized final bill with no manual tallying
Customer benefit: Faster, error-free front-desk operations
Suggested screen-recording sequence: Reservation → check-in → folio (charge added) → checkout screen with total

## Workflow: Guest Orders Food via QR Code
Starting screen: A phone showing a QR code scan
Action: Guest browses the digital menu and places an order
Next screen/action: Cut to the kitchen display — order appears instantly
Result: Kitchen starts preparing immediately, no paper order slip
Customer benefit: Faster, more modern guest service
Suggested screen-recording sequence: Phone scan → menu browse → order placed → kitchen display order appears (split-screen or quick cut works well)

## Workflow: Housekeeping Updates a Room from Their Phone
Starting screen: Housekeeping mobile app on a phone
Action: Staff marks a room "clean" after finishing
Next screen/action: Cut to front-desk screen — room status updates live
Result: The room becomes available to sell again immediately
Customer benefit: Front desk always has accurate room-readiness information
Suggested screen-recording sequence: Phone (mark clean) → front-desk room grid (status flips) — works well as a split-screen or quick cut

## Workflow: Manager Reviews the Day's Business
Starting screen: Dashboard
Action: Open a couple of key reports (e.g., daily report, occupancy trend)
Next screen/action: Scroll through populated charts/numbers
Result: A clear read on how the property performed
Customer benefit: Business visibility without spreadsheets
Suggested screen-recording sequence: Dashboard → report page → chart/number close-ups

# 15-Second Video Structure

0-3 sec: Hook — a visual of hotel chaos (paper registers, sticky notes, a phone call about a double-booking) or a simple on-screen question like "Still running your hotel on paper and WhatsApp?"

3-8 sec: Show the product performing its main action — e.g., a booking coming in from an OTA and instantly appearing in InnFlo, availability syncing automatically.

8-12 sec: Show the result — the dashboard or reports screen with clean, organized data; or a QR order landing on the kitchen display.

12-15 sec: Value proposition + CTA concept — "One system. Every part of your hotel." + a call to action like "See InnFlo" (no specific claim beyond what's verified).

# 30-Second Video Structure

0-4 sec: Hook — the same problem framing (fragmented, manual hotel operations).
4-10 sec: Introduce InnFlo as one connected system — quick montage of reservations, folio, housekeeping.
10-18 sec: Demo the channel manager syncing an OTA booking automatically — the "no more overbooking" moment.
18-24 sec: Demo a guest-facing moment — QR ordering or the direct booking website — to show modern guest experience.
24-30 sec: End on the reports/dashboard screen (business visibility) + CTA.

# 60-Second Explainer Structure

0-8 sec: Problem — a small hotel owner juggling paper, spreadsheets, and phone calls; missed bookings and overbookings.
8-18 sec: Introduce InnFlo as the single system that replaces all of it.
18-30 sec: Walk through the front-desk/guest journey — booking comes in (from OTA or direct site) → check-in → folio charges build up → checkout.
30-42 sec: Show the operational side — housekeeping mobile app updating a room, kitchen display receiving a QR order.
42-52 sec: Show the business intelligence side — dashboard and a couple of reports.
52-60 sec: Value proposition recap + CTA (e.g., "book a walkthrough").

# Good Urdu Explanation Concepts

Do not attempt formal Urdu translation here — instead, explain the concepts simply for a non-technical Pakistani audience:

- **The core idea**: Instead of managing bookings, guests, bills, and staff separately (on paper, in registers, over WhatsApp), everything lives in one system that all your staff can see and use together.
- **OTA sync**: Explain in terms of "your rooms on Booking.com/Agoda automatically update the moment someone books anywhere — so you never accidentally sell the same room twice."
- **Direct booking site**: Explain as "your own website where guests can book directly — you don't pay any commission on those bookings, unlike Booking.com or Agoda."
- **QR ordering**: Explain as "guests scan a code at the table with their own phone and order — no waiting for a waiter to bring a menu."
- **Housekeeping app**: Explain as "your cleaning staff update room status straight from their own phone, and the front desk sees it immediately — works even where the Wi-Fi is weak."
- **Reports**: Explain as "at the end of the day, the owner can see exactly how much money came in, how many rooms were sold, and where — without doing it by hand."

Terms that should probably stay in English even in an Urdu-language video, since they're already common usage:
- dashboard
- booking / booking engine
- WhatsApp
- QR code
- app
- invoice
- online travel agency / OTA (may still need a one-line Urdu explanation the first time it's used)

# Screens Not Worth Showing

- Any raw database/admin tooling, the internal super-admin panel (used by InnFlo's own team to provision hotels), or any developer/debug view — not customer-relevant and could look unpolished or confusing.
- The Roles & Permissions v2 configuration (currently unused/Phase 2) — showing it could create the impression of a feature that isn't actually enforced yet.
- The Maintenance and Inventory/Cashbook modules — functional but earlier-stage; showing them prominently risks setting expectations the product doesn't fully meet yet.
- Any screen showing rate plan modifiers (percentage/fixed adjustments) as if they affect pricing — this is a known bug and would misrepresent the product.
- The guest messaging/unified inbox screen, specifically anything implying live WhatsApp sending — this integration is not functional yet.
- Any screen implying online payment collection on the booking engine (e.g., a card entry form) — this does not exist; showing it would misrepresent the product.
- Raw audit logs or system health/technical monitoring screens — not meaningful to a customer audience.

# Demo Risks

- **Never show real guest data** — names, phone numbers, ID documents, payment details, or stay history of real hotel guests. Use clearly fictional demo data only.
- **Never show real staff login credentials, API keys, or environment configuration.**
- **Never show real payment or financial account details** (bank account numbers, actual transaction records).
- **Do not demo or imply WhatsApp messages are actually being sent** — the underlying integration is a non-functional placeholder; recording this would show fake or broken behavior.
- **Do not demo online payment/checkout on the booking engine** — this functionality doesn't exist; attempting to show it would either fail on camera or misrepresent the product.
- **Avoid demoing the rate plan modifier feature as if it changes pricing** — it currently doesn't, due to a known bug.
- **Avoid the internal super-admin panel entirely** — it's an internal tool for InnFlo's own team, not something a hotel customer would ever see, and could expose information about other hotel accounts on the platform.
- **Double-check any on-screen numbers/reports** before recording to ensure they're demo/seed data, not a real property's actual financials.
