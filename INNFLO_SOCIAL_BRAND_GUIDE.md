# Innflo Social Brand Guide

Use this guide for every Innflo social-media graphic. It defines the brand’s visual identity without prescribing a fixed layout or limiting creative direction.

## Brand identity

- Brand name: **Innflo**
- Innflo is a calm, modern hotel-management platform for independent hospitality properties.
- The brand should feel warm, capable, thoughtful, credible and easy to understand.
- The visual style should combine editorial hospitality design with modern SaaS clarity.

## Logo

The Innflo logo is the primary brand identifier and **must appear on every post**.

- It may appear in any corner that suits the composition.
- Keep it clearly visible, correctly proportioned and comfortably away from the edge.
- Use the original repository asset; never redraw, regenerate, distort or recolour it.
- Use the clay version on light backgrounds and the reverse version on dark backgrounds.
- The logo should be noticeable without competing with the main message.

## Colour palette

| Colour | Hex | Typical use |
|---|---:|---|
| Coral | `#E0532B` | Primary brand accent, emphasis and CTA |
| Dark coral | `#C2431F` | Strong accents and headings |
| Deep coral | `#9E3417` | Darker coral text and contrast |
| Warm paper | `#F5EBE4` | Warm editorial background |
| Soft mist | `#FAF8F4` | Light background and clean surfaces |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | UI surfaces and contrast |
| Dark ink | `#211E1A` | Primary text and dark backgrounds |
| Soft ink | `#4A453E` | Supporting text |
| Muted ink | `#938C81` | Secondary details only |

The palette may be used in light, dark or coral-led compositions. Not every post needs a paper background or dot-grid texture.

## Typography

- **Fraunces**: Display headlines and expressive editorial typography.
- **Inter**: Supporting copy, labels, CTAs and product-interface text.
- Fallbacks: Georgia for Fraunces; system sans-serif for Inter.
- Headlines should be confident and readable without occupying the entire canvas.
- Keep supporting text short and legible at mobile-feed size.

### Signature headline treatment

Social graphics should follow the expressive headline style used on the Innflo marketing site:

- Set the main headline in Fraunces using Dark Ink, Warm Paper or another high-contrast base colour.
- Highlight one meaningful word or short phrase in a contrasting brand colour and *italic Fraunces*.
- Coral is the preferred highlight on light backgrounds; Warm Paper or Coral may be used on dark backgrounds.
- Use only one highlighted segment per headline so the emphasis remains intentional.
- Choose the highlighted word for meaning, not decoration.
- Compose line breaks deliberately so the highlighted word feels integrated into the headline.
- Keep the remaining headline visually calm; avoid mixing several fonts, colours or emphasis effects.

Example structure:

> Know who’s arriving.  
> Before they reach the *desk.*

In the finished design, `desk` would use the contrasting brand colour and italic Fraunces.

## Design language

Preferred qualities:

- Warm editorial hospitality
- Modern SaaS polish
- Strong visual hierarchy
- Intentional whitespace
- Clean typography
- Restrained depth and soft shadows
- Thoughtful asymmetry
- Terracotta, paper, ink and natural material cues
- One clear focal point at feed size
- Product-led or concept-led storytelling

Avoid:

- Generic blue SaaS gradients
- Neon colours
- Glassmorphism
- Luxury-gold clichés
- Generic corporate stock aesthetics
- Overcrowded layouts
- Tiny unreadable UI
- Giant headlines that overwhelm the design
- Repeating the same composition for every post
- Designs that look like wireframes, presentation slides or template specimens

## Imagery

- **Do not use AI-generated people, faces, hands, bodies, employees, guests, founders or customers.**
- Avoid AI-generated photorealistic imagery whenever possible.
- Never imply that an artificial person uses or endorses Innflo.
- Prefer real owned or properly licensed hotel interiors, architecture, rooms, landscapes and hospitality objects.
- Product-led, typography-led and brand-graphic designs are encouraged when real photography is unavailable.
- AI may be used sparingly for non-human abstract textures, patterns or clearly stylised decorative elements.
- Do not generate logos, important text or product interfaces inside an AI image.

## Product interface and floating pills

- Product UI should feel polished, credible and connected to a real Innflo feature.
- It may be simplified or enlarged for social-media readability.
- Never expose real hotel or guest information. Use clearly synthetic demonstration data.
- Do not invent features, integrations, performance results, testimonials or statistics.

Floating UI pills are an approved visual preference:

- Use them as supporting accents, not the whole design.
- Prefer one to three pills when they improve the composition.
- Keep them visually connected to the main interface or concept.
- Use short, readable product states or labels.
- Avoid random, duplicated, cropped or disconnected pills.
- Do not use revenue, occupancy or performance figures as marketing proof unless explicitly approved.

## Platform formats

| Format | Size |
|---|---:|
| Instagram/Facebook portrait | `1080 × 1350` |
| Instagram/LinkedIn square | `1200 × 1200` |
| Story/Reel/TikTok vertical | `1080 × 1920` |
| Wide social/link graphic | `1200 × 630` |

- Recompose the design for each ratio rather than stretching it.
- Keep essential text, product UI and the Innflo logo safely away from platform overlays and canvas edges.
- Always check readability at mobile-feed size before publishing.
