# Week 01 Campaign — Introducing Innflo

## Campaign idea

Introduce Innflo as a calm, modern hotel-management platform for independent hospitality properties in Pakistan.

The week should help hotel owners and managers understand:

- What Innflo is
- Why it is being built
- Which everyday hotel problems it addresses
- What the product feels like

This is an introduction, not a hard-sales campaign. Keep the messaging clear, credible and human.

## Audience

Owners, managers and front-desk teams at independent hotels, guesthouses and other hospitality properties in Pakistan.

## Creative direction

Read `INNFLO_SOCIAL_BRAND_GUIDE.md` before creating any post.

Use creative freedom within that visual language. The seven posts should feel related but should not repeat the same composition. Prefer strong typography, brand graphics, credible product-interface visuals and real licensed non-human hospitality photography where useful.

Do not use AI-generated people or faces. The Innflo logo must appear on every post.

## The seven posts

### Day 1 — Meet Innflo

**Idea:** Introduce the brand and what it exists to do.

**Core message:** Innflo brings essential hotel operations into one calm, connected system designed for independent properties.

**Creative opportunity:** A confident brand-opening graphic that establishes the visual identity. It may be typography-led, product-led or a combination of both.

### Day 2 — The scattered front desk

**Idea:** Show the operational friction created by registers, spreadsheets, calls and chat threads.

**Core message:** Important hotel information should not be scattered across disconnected tools.

**Creative opportunity:** Interpret the contrast between operational fragments and a calmer, organised flow. Avoid copying the WhatsApp interface or logo.

### Day 3 — Today’s arrivals

**Idea:** Introduce the front-desk view of expected arrivals, departures and room readiness.

**Core message:** Managers should be able to understand the day ahead from one clear operating view.

**Verified reference:** `apps/marketing/src/components/features/HomeTabMockups.tsx`

**Creative opportunity:** Use a polished, enlarged product-interface interpretation with optional connected floating pills. Keep essential information readable.

### Day 4 — Room readiness

**Idea:** Share a useful hotel-operations lesson about keeping housekeeping and the front desk aligned.

**Core message:** Clear task ownership and visible room status help the desk understand what is ready for the next arrival.

**Verified references:**

- `apps/web/src/services/housekeeping.ts`
- `apps/web/src/pages/housekeeping/HousekeepingPage.tsx`
- `apps/web/src/pages/housekeeping/HousekeepingMobilePage.tsx`

**Creative opportunity:** This can be educational, diagrammatic or interface-led. Use only verified statuses such as Pending, In Progress and Completed.

### Day 5 — From booking to ready again

**Idea:** Show how a reservation moves through a connected hotel workflow.

**Core message:** A direct booking can move through the front desk, checkout and housekeeping flow before the room becomes ready again.

**Verified reference:** `apps/marketing/src/components/ProductCockpit.tsx`

**Creative opportunity:** A carousel, visual sequence or connected interface story. Do not imply that automatic OTA synchronization is currently live.

### Day 6 — Why we are building Innflo

**Idea:** Explain the brand’s manager-first point of view.

**Core message:** Independent hospitality properties in Pakistan deserve capable software shaped around their daily operational reality.

**Verified reference:** `apps/marketing/src/pages/About.tsx`

**Creative opportunity:** A thoughtful brand-story post using expressive typography, product details or real approved brand imagery. Do not use an AI-generated founder or team photograph.

### Day 7 — Calm operations, warmer hospitality

**Idea:** Close the week with a hospitality insight rather than another product pitch.

**Core message:** Better operational clarity leaves more attention for the guest experience.

**Creative opportunity:** Use a strong editorial concept, real licensed hospitality-object or interior photography, a custom diagram, or a restrained product-and-brand composition. Treat the message as brand philosophy, not a measurable performance claim.

## Product and claim boundaries

- Use only product capabilities supported by the cited repository references.
- Automatic two-way OTA/channel synchronization is in development and must not be shown as live.
- Do not invent customer results, testimonials, ratings, revenue, occupancy improvements or time-saving statistics.
- Use synthetic demonstration data in product-interface visuals; never expose real hotel or guest information.
- Do not assume a public URL, social handle, pricing offer or link-in-bio CTA unless it is provided separately.

## Primary format

Create the Instagram/Facebook portrait version first at `1080 × 1350`. Other platform formats can be created after the post’s creative direction is approved.
